Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question to view guidance
Use the Global Search input in the top navigation bar or the search and filter controls in the Regional Case Tracking and Pending & Overdue Cases sections. You can search directly by Case ID (e.g., CASE-2026-0143), Institution Name, AICTE Affiliation Code, or Complaint Subject.
Overdue cases are identified with prominent red status pills and red row accents across the dashboard. Navigate to the Pending & Overdue Cases section and select "Overdue Cases" in the Due Date or Case Status filter dropdown to isolate cases that have exceeded their statutory resolution window.
A potential overlap alert indicates that two or more complaints share significant common parameters—such as being filed against the same regional institution, involving cross-bureau handling (e.g., Regulation Bureau and PGRC), or referencing identical grievance topics. It alerts officers that coordinated handling is recommended to avoid duplicative inquiries.
No. Matching complaint subjects or common institution codes do not automatically prove duplication. Multiple stakeholders (such as students, faculty, or applicants) may submit distinct legitimate grievances regarding the same broad issue, or different Bureaus may have independent statutory jurisdictions. The reviewing regional officer must conduct human review before any administrative linkage is established.
Navigate to the Regional Reports section. There you will find summary metrics for total, resolved, pending, and overdue cases, accompanied by visual charts (Case Status Distribution doughnut and Bureau/Cell bar distribution) as well as an institution-wise complaint performance table. You can also export this data directly to CSV.
Chronological records of actions—such as case status updates, institution responses, and assignments—are displayed in the Recent Activities section. Actionable deadline alerts, overlap notices, and priority updates are presented in the Notifications section and via the notification bell icon in the top header.

Dashboard User Guide

1. Regional Jurisdiction: Verify your active assigned region using the region selector in the top navigation header. All dashboard analytics and rosters correspond to the active region.
2. Deadline Surveillance: Check the Pending & Overdue Cases roster daily. Pay immediate attention to cases marked with urgent red badges or approaching 7-day deadlines.
3. Cross-Bureau Coordination: Use the Overlap Alerts module to examine complaints with matching parameters side-by-side before dispatching duplicate inquiries to institutions.
4. Analytical Reporting: Generate and review monthly institution-wise complaint ratios under Regional Reports to support regulatory oversight.
