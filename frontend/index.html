About EduVision

One Platform. Complete Transparency.

EduVision is an integrated solution by AICTE to monitor and manage institution-related complaints and regulatory actions. It brings together all Bureaus/Cells, enables real-time tracking, detects overlapping cases and ensures better coordination, faster disposal and data-driven decisions.
