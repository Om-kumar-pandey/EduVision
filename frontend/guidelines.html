AICTE Grievance Guidelines Handbook (2026 Edition)

Official comprehensive guidelines on procedures, timelines, appeals, and regulatory compliance.

1. Step-by-Step Filing Procedure

  • Exhaust Internal Remedies: Complainants are encouraged to initially represent grievances before the Institutional Grievance Redressal Committee (SGRC/CGRC).
  • Provide Specifics: Include exact course name, registration number, semester, and dates when referencing academic or financial disputes.
  • Permitted Attachments: Official fee receipts, mark sheets, official communications, emails, and circulars in PDF, JPG, PNG or DOCX formats (max 5MB each).
  • Resolution Timelines: AICTE regulations mandate an initial evaluation within 7 working days and standard resolution within 15 to 30 calendar days.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who is eligible to submit a grievance on EduVision?
Any enrolled student, teacher, head of department, or administrative member belonging to any AICTE-approved technical institution across all Indian States and Union Territories can register and file grievances.
Is my identity revealed to the college authorities?
Grievances are investigated objectively. In sensitive categories such as harassment or ragging, confidential complainant safeguards are enforced pursuant to AICTE anti-harassment regulations.
How can I track the progress of my filed complaint?
Every filed grievance is assigned a unique tracking number (e.g. CMP-2026-000103). You can track live milestones on the Track Complaint page or through your authenticated My Complaints dashboard.
What happens if an institution fails to respond?
If an institution does not submit remarks within the statutory 14-day inquiry window, the matter is automatically escalated to the AICTE Regional Officer and Central Ombudsman for enforcement measures.
